Our Story

Rooted in Tradition. Alive with Flavour.

We opened our doors in 2011 with one belief that great food is never accidental. Every dish on our menu is a conversation between the seasons and the people we cook for. Our kitchen is led by Chef Marco Delacroix, whose 20 years across Lyon and London have shaped a style that's both deeply classical and refreshingly alive.
