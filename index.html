Welcome to Enkie Food Restaurant

At Enkie food is more than a meal, it’s comfort, community, and culture on a plate. We specialize in authentic Nigerian delicacies and flavorful continental dishes, prepared fresh daily with the best ingredients. Whether you’re craving pepper soup and swallow, jollof and grilled chicken, or something light and quick, Enkie has something for everyone. Our goal is simple: great food, great service, and a welcoming space where family, friends, and colleagues can come together. Dine-in, takeaway, and delivery available.
